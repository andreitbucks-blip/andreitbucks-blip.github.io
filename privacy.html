Time Rift - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Time Rift (package com.fratilaandrei.timerift), developed by Fratila Andrei ("we"), handles information. If you have any question, contact us at andreitbucks@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no sign-in. Your game progress and settings (records, difficulty, volume, deck choices) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch them for an in-game reward) and occasional full-screen ads between battles. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. This processing is done by Google under its own policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is shown, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and change your choice later from the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

3. In-app purchase (Google Play)

The optional one-time purchase (removes ads and unlocks premium modes) is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made so the game can unlock it.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the controls described above or contact Google. If you live in the EU/EEA you may also contact your local data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP).

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
